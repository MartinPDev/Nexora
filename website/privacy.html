Nexora Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Nexora respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how users can contact Nexora about privacy questions.

Information We Collect

Nexora may collect information users provide directly, including name, email address, trading experience level, account registration details, and support inquiries.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to manage early access registration, provide platform updates, improve Nexora services, respond to support requests, and operate the website or application.

Trading and Exchange Data

Future versions of Nexora may connect with supported crypto exchanges through user-provided API access. Nexora will use connected exchange data only to provide platform features, trading tools, analytics, automation, and account-related functionality.

Data Security

Nexora is designed with security in mind, including controlled access handling and platform-level protections. No online system can guarantee complete security.

Third-Party Services

Nexora may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, payment processing, email communication, and exchange connectivity.

User Choices

Users may request updates or removal of their early access information by contacting Nexora.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Nexora develops, launches new features, or adds additional services.

Contact

Privacy contact information will be added before public launch.
